# Personalsite agent instructions

The site represents Aria's actual work and voice. Primary evidence beats generic positioning or
design advice.

## Voice and positioning

- Read the relevant published Medium pieces, CollabVault source material, and Aria's current public
  profiles before proposing identity or positioning copy.
- Current positioning center: “Companies know they need AI, but they don’t know what it should look
  like in practice. I help answer that question, then build the answer.”
- Do not invent preferences, process claims, or personality traits. Aria has not said she prefers
  writing before calls.
- Do not use “unglamorous.” Do not manufacture slogans, symmetrical fragments, “mic-drop” lines, or
  a service list unless the source material earns them.
- Preserve ordinary sentence rhythm. Voice skills are warnings and comparison aids, not authority
  over Aria's real writing.

## Review and external-change boundary

- For copy review, show current text beside proposed text and explain the evidence for each material
  change.
- A review request does not authorize editing the live site, LinkedIn, Medium, X, or another public
  surface. Prepare the artifact and wait for explicit approval.
- Artifact treatment follows the user's request. “Utilitarian” or “don't style the doc” overrides
  frontend and design doctrine.
- When implementation is authorized, verify the actual rendered page at its user-visible
  breakpoints. A build or commit is not visual proof.
